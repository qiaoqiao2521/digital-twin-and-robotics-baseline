=== Alexandria Tools ===

  faa-gov/aviation-records/make_model_lookup
    Fleet-size questions (`how many SR22s are registered in Florida?`), or to find the canonical registry manufacturer/model spelling and MMS code before other lookups. Manufacturer and model match as prefixes.

  faa-gov/aviation-records/aircraft_search
    Find the tail numbers an operator owns or every aircraft with a given serial number, then call `aircraft` for the detail record. Owner names match as a prefix on the registry's upper-case name. For dealer certificates use `dealer_search`.

  faa-gov/aviation-records/aircraft
    Look up who owns a US-registered aircraft or what a tail number is. Unassigned numbers are not_found; malformed numbers are invalid_input.

  faa-gov/aviation-records/airworthiness_directive
    Read the details of an airworthiness directive (or any DRS document) found with `airworthiness_directive_search`. Unknown keys are not_found.

  faa-gov/aviation-records/airworthiness_directive_search
    Find the ADs and related documents for an aircraft make or model, a part or a topic. Filter with `status: Current` for in-force documents; page with `offset`.

  wikipedia/articles/lookup
    Read the current revision of a Wikipedia (or sister-project) article by exact title: full wikitext (or HTML with body), abstract, categories, redirects, connected Wikidata QID, license, and revision credibility signals. Use structured-contents instead when you want infobox fields, sections or references already parsed. From https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin pass name `Berlin` and project `dewiki`. Not a search: titles must match exactly.

  wikipedia/articles/structured-contents
    Read a Wikipedia article pre-parsed into JSON: one-line description, abstract, infobox fields, sections as paragraphs and lists with links, references and tables. Best for extracting facts such as a person's birth date or a city's population without parsing HTML. Beta upstream, with the most complete coverage on en, de, fr, es, pt, it, nl, cy and id Wikipedia. Exact title, as for articles/lookup.

  wikipedia/reference/languages
    List the language codes Wikimedia projects use, with names and writing direction, to choose the language option or build a project identifier. Codes are two or three letters, sometimes with a suffix (zh-yue). Free.

  wikipedia/reference/projects
    List the Wikimedia projects available for lookup, to find the project identifier for a language and project type, such as `ptwiki` for Portuguese Wikipedia or `frwiktionary`. Free.

  wikipedia/wikidata/item
    Read a Wikidata item by QID: labels, descriptions and aliases in every language, all statements (property values with qualifiers and references) and links to its article on each wiki. Use for structured facts such as dates, identifiers and relationships, or to find an entity's article title in another language through sitelinks. Beta upstream; a very large item can fail.
